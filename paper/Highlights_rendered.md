# Highlights

- In the 2017 haor flood, Boro area fell by about a third while yield barely moved
- BBS damage reports and satellite greenness loss support this loss
- The 2016 and 2022 flood years showed no comparable fall, so no general rule holds
- Exploratory results point to flood timing, a hypothesis that rests on 2017
- Yield alone can miss large flood losses, so track area and production too
