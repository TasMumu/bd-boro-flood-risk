# Journal choice

Step 6 of the analysis plan, 31 October 2026.

**Recommended: International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction (Elsevier).**

The paper's main point is about measuring disaster losses, and the journal already
publishes flood and crop work from Bangladesh (Dey et al., 2021; Rahman et al., 2025). It
is hybrid, so there is a free subscription route, which matters because the paper has no
funding. The manuscript already uses Elsevier's front matter, highlights and declarations.

| Journal | Impact factor (approx.) | Model | Fit |

| Regional Environmental Change | 3.4–4.3 | Hybrid | Good: regional impacts and adaptation |
| Agricultural Systems | 6.2 | Hybrid | Possible: stronger on systems than on one hazard |

Impact factors come from third-party listings, which disagree, so confirm them and the
author guidelines on the journal's own page before submitting. The journal's word and
abstract limits were not confirmed; the abstract is 180 words and the manuscript is about
9,500 words including tables and references.
